# The Gilded Table

A complete original turn-based duel card game for desktop browsers. You are the Gilded Warden, dueling the Ash Rival across a gilded table for control of the court. Each duelist starts with 30 life and builds mana one gem per turn (to a maximum of 10) to play cards from a 24-card deck of four suits — Ember, Tide, Leaf and Gale.

The four suits form a cycle of Court Advantage: Ember beats Leaf, Tide beats Ember, Leaf beats Tide, and Gale beats nothing. When you play a card whose suit beats the suit of the rival's last played card, you deal +3 bonus damage. Cards come in two kinds — attacks that deal their power as damage, and wards that raise a shield to absorb incoming hits. Reduce the rival to 0 life to win the court.

## How to play

Run `node serve.ts` and open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. Click a card in your hand or press 1-7 to play it, press Space or click END TURN to end your turn, R to restart, and M to mute. The whole table fits in one 1280x720 frame.
